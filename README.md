# 🏜 14-0: Dakar Rally Draft

Draft 5 legendary Dakar Rally competitors across 6 eras and try to win **every one of the 14 timed stages** of a 4,748 km rally raid.

**84 legends · 6 eras · 14 stages · 1 goal: 14-0.**

## The Dakar

The Dakar Rally is the world's most famous rally-raid event — 4,748 km of special stages across the Saudi Arabian desert, run by ASO since 1978. Unlike stage rally, Dakar is a navigation-based endurance contest: a prologue, 13 stages, a rest day, and three 48-hour marathon stages where competitors navigate by roadbook with no assistance.

2026 was the 48th edition — won by **Nasser Al-Attiyah** (his 6th) with **Luciano Benavides** taking the bike class.

## How to play

1. Pick **5 legends** from any era tab
2. Watch the era + class synergy bonuses build
3. Simulate all 14 stages
4. Chase the perfect 14-0

## Mechanics

- **Era synergy** — stacking multiple legends from the same era grants bonus OVR
- **Rally team bonus** — mixing CAR / BIKE / TRUCK classes rewards a full team (Dakar is a team event: a car, a bike and a truck)
- **Terrain matters** — `gravel` (neutral), `dune` (-0.03, navigation chaos), `marathon` (+0.02, rewards endurance)
- Total bonus budget is capped at **6 OVR**, so you choose how to spend it
